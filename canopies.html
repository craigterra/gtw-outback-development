CANOPIES / RANGE PREVIEW

Room inside. Room behind.

Choose half or three-quarter canopy coverage, then plan the doors, finish, interior and exterior fit-out.

WHY WE LEAVE ROOM OUT BACK

Some gear belongs outside.

We’ve found a short open tray section is a great place for firewood and dirty gear, keeping it out of your clean canopy space.

Our canopy range focuses on half and three-quarter coverage. That leaves room to plan spare wheels and other exterior gear around the tray, rather than automatically hanging everything off the back of a full-length canopy.

Heavy items mounted far behind the rear axle can add rearward leverage. Rear-mounted equipment can also crowd access when you’re hitching a trailer. We plan the final layout around the vehicle, its loaded weights, tow hitch and the way you use it.

Short sideboards and a rear drop-down gate are options for the open tray section. Final dimensions, mounting and clearances are confirmed for each build.
