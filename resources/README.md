# GTW Outback resources library

Approved reusable brand assets live here. Keep original masters versioned; export lightweight website copies into `assets/`. The build publishes `assets/` only, so this library stays out of the deployed website.

The canopy camp image is an AI-generated brand concept, not a product photograph or a dimensional/engineering reference. It depicts a white single-cab 79 Series with the canopy, rear ladder, spare, black rooftop tent and passenger-side freestanding awning in an outback landscape. Preserve the approved equipment arrangement when making variants. Keep the original master; give edits a new version number and record the approval here.

Full generation prompts and earlier working variants are archived in the project workspace under `Branding Collateral/Website hero` and are not published with the site.

## Adding resources

Use `images/core/` for approved master imagery. Add logos, product references or other categories as needed, with a short entry identifying source, intended use and approval status. Do not add credentials, customer details or unapproved supplier assets to this public repository.
