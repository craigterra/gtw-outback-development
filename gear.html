YOUR SETUP QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

Do you offer standalone ute toolboxes?

Our range focuses on canopies, trays and modular canopy fit-outs. We do not offer standalone ute toolboxes.

Do we supply 12V electrical systems?

There are plenty of great 12V electrical systems available. We want you to choose the system that suits your budget, your requirements and the way you use your setup.

We can arrange installation of a system you supply by qualified installers as part of one of our custom tray and canopy packages.
