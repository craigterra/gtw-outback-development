Our story

We’ve tried
a few setups.

For me, it started heading west for work. A swag beside the ute, or tucked under the trailer tray when it was wet or frosty. There was no electric fridge in those days. You ate the food that needed to stay cold first, and when the beer got warm, it was time for more ice in the esky on the next trip back to town.

Then came the family, and the swags made way for tents. Until the siren song of the camper trailer called: a bit more comfort, a bit more room, and plenty more trips away.

We loved those trips. The long setups and drying out wet canvas once we got home got old, so we moved on. Our next setup was a big off-road caravan, with bunks for the kids and room for the whole family.

But families change. The kids outgrew the bunks and got busy doing their own thing. What suited us then isn’t what suits us now.

Less setup.
More time out.

Currently, our perfect setup is a canopy on the ute. We throw the food in and go. Free camps and remote spots are our go-to. Somewhere quiet to pull up, settle in and switch off.

We’ve kept trying things over the years and changing our setup so it’s easier to get away more often.

We still want the adventure. We just don’t think camping needs to be hard work.

Gear has to
earn its place.

Like plenty of campers, we’ve bought our share from the big outdoor retailers. We’ve tried the cheap gear that broke and needed replacing.

We’ve put our share of broken awnings, chairs and other casualties in the bin after Easter. We’ve had things rattle loose, fridges come unplugged on the corrugations, gear rub through and welds break. We’ve had our share of the challenges that come your way. We’ve forgotten the gas bottle and left the tent pegs behind, too.

Nothing like arriving at camp, opening a tent full of black mould, and making a 700 km round trip to buy another one. Ask me how I know.

That’s why we appreciate quality gear and a well-organised setup.

We’ve bought the clever little gadgets that seemed essential, then spent most of their lives taking up space.

Now, we’d rather have fewer things that do their job well. Practical, durable gear that makes the setup easier and lasts trip after trip.

We know everyone starts somewhere. When you’re working out whether camping is for you, you’re also working out what you need. There’s no single right setup, and it doesn’t all have to happen at once.

For those who know this life is for them, GTW Outback is about choosing gear worth keeping.

From our setup
to yours.

The standard is personal: gear we’d choose for our own setup, use ourselves and be willing to stand behind.

That’s what drives GTW Outback. Quality gear, practical storage and support from real people who share your passion for getting out there.

Our no-fuss promise belongs at the heart of that. When you need help, we want a straightforward conversation with someone who understands why your gear matters.

Our promise to you is simple: gear to get you out and back, trip after trip.

Gear that works.
Out and back.
